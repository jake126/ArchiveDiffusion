# Research Questions

## Primary question

Can diffusion-based restoration improve archival film still quality while preserving the visual texture and cinematic identity of the source material?

## Supporting questions

1. How well does a compact diffusion model learn a narrow visual domain such as public-domain film stills?
2. What degradation types are most learnable and most visually meaningful?
3. Does optimising reconstruction quality lead to over-smoothed or over-modernised outputs?
4. Can a restoration pipeline expose a controllable trade-off between clarity and archival authenticity?
5. Can faster sampling strategies preserve acceptable quality for local restoration workflows?
6. Are generated/restored outputs too similar to training examples, suggesting memorisation?
